# Billing and Plans

Fernwood has three plans. **Free**: $0/month, up to 3 active projects, up to
5 teammates. **Pro**: $9 per user/month, unlimited projects, up to 50
teammates. **Team**: $15 per user/month, unlimited projects, unlimited
teammates, priority support. Paying annually on any paid plan gives you 2
months free — you pay for 10 months and get 12.
